# Curcumin-Target-Identification-Drug-Druggability-Assessment
1. Project Overview:
   
This project focuses on exploring the potential molecular targets of Curcumin, a naturally occurring polyphenolic compound, using the ChEMBL bioactivity database.

The study aims to identify reported protein targets associated with Curcumin and investigate their potential druggability based on available bioactivity and target-related information.

2. Objectives:
   
Retrieve Curcumin-related information from the ChEMBL database.
Identify protein targets associated with Curcumin.
Explore available bioactivity data, including IC50 and Ki values.
Investigate the potential druggability of identified targets.
Understand the application of chemical databases in computational drug discovery.

3. Tools and Resources:
   
ChEMBL Database
Web-based bioactivity data retrieval
Molecular target information
Spreadsheet software for data organization (if used)

4. Methodology:

Search for Curcumin in the ChEMBL database.
Retrieve its compound information and ChEMBL identifier.
Explore associated bioactivity records.
Identify reported molecular targets.
Examine available activity measurements and assay details.
Evaluate target druggability using relevant supporting evidence.

5. Results and Observations:

The identified targets, associated bioactivity measurements, assay information and druggability evidence are documented in the results section.

6. Conclusion:

This practical demonstrates how chemical bioactivity databases can support molecular target exploration and early-stage computational drug discovery research.

7. Database Reference:

ChEMBL: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/

8. Disclaimer:

Database associations and reported bioactivity measurements do not independently establish direct target engagement, therapeutic efficacy or clinical relevance.
